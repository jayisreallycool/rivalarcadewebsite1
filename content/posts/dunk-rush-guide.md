Dunk Rush is a 60-second flick-basketball game. It's easy to sink a few baskets and surprisingly hard to put up a big score. This guide covers how points work and how to get more of them.

## The controls

- Touch anywhere and **drag up**. A longer pull shoots farther — watch the power meter.
- **Slide left or right** while you pull to aim.
- **Let go** to shoot. Pull back down to cancel a shot you don't like.

## How shots are scored

| Shot | Points |
|---|---|
| Bank shot or rim shot | 1 |
| Swish (nothing but net) | 2 |
| Flame-ball swish | 6 |

Three swishes in a row lights the **flame ball**, and a swish with the flame ball is worth six. That's the whole game: swishes are worth double on their own, and they're the only way to reach the six-pointer.

## Find your pull length

Every great Dunk Rush run starts with consistent power. Spend a couple of practice runs learning exactly how far to pull for a swish from the starting spot. Once that's muscle memory, you only need to think about aim.

## Don't chase every shot

A rushed bank shot is worth 1 and breaks nothing — but a rushed miss ends a swish streak you were building toward the flame ball. If a shot feels wrong, pull back down to cancel it. You lose a moment, not a streak.

## The moving hoop

Partway through the round the hoop starts moving. Two habits help:

1. **Lead the hoop.** Aim where it will be, not where it is.
2. **Shoot at the ends of its path.** The hoop slows as it changes direction, which gives you a steadier target.

## Clutch time

The last ten seconds of every timed RivalArcade round are **CLUTCH ×2** — and it stacks with ON FIRE. Make sure you're shooting, not resetting, when the clock hits ten.
