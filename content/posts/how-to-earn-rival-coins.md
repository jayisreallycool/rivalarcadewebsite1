Rival Coins (RC) are RivalArcade's cosmetic currency. You earn them by playing and spend them on gear for your 3D avatar. Here's every way to earn them.

## First: what Rival Coins are not

Rival Coins have **no cash value**. They can't be bought, withdrawn, sold or sent to another player, and they're **never staked** on a match — every Rival Coin contest is free to enter and the prizes are paid by the house. They only buy cosmetics and the season pass VIP track, and no cosmetic changes how a game plays.

## Every way to earn

- **Welcome grant:** 1,000 RC when you create an account.
- **Daily login:** 50 RC, plus 10 RC for each consecutive day, up to 100 RC a day.
- **Daily challenges:** a fresh set every day, such as finishing matches or hitting a target in one game.
- **Weekly challenges:** bigger goals that reset every Monday at 00:00 UTC — win 10 duels (600 RC), finish 20 competitive matches, play 3 cup runs or play on 5 different days (450 RC each).
- **Practice:** 4–12 RC per valid run depending on your score, up to 300 RC a day.
- **1v1 duels:** 20 RC for a win, 10 each for a tie and 3 for playing it out.
- **Live Arena:** a 70 RC pool split 35 / 21 / 14 across the top three.
- **Cups:** a 600 RC pool shared by the top seven, plus the cup's cosmetic for the champion.
- **Achievements:** 29 of them, from First Victory (100 RC) to Level 100 and Rival Elite (2,000 RC each).

## The daily cap

Gameplay rewards are capped at **2,000 RC per UTC day**. The cap keeps the economy healthy and means nobody can farm their way past everyone else overnight. Rewarded matches against the same opponent are also limited to three a day, which stops friends trading wins.

## How long does a Mythic take?

Store prices follow rarity:

| Rarity | Price (RC) |
|---|---|
| Common | 500–1,000 |
| Uncommon | 1,500–3,000 |
| Rare | 5,000–9,000 |
| Epic | 15,000–25,000 |
| Legendary | 40,000–65,000 |
| Mythic | 75,000–90,000 |

RivalArcade's own economy model puts a Mythic at roughly seven to eight weeks of regular, active play. That's deliberate: if you see someone in the Aurora Holo Coat, they earned it.

## Tips

- Claim your login every day — the streak is worth up to double.
- Daily challenges stack toward the Taskmaster weekly (claim 12).
- Cups pay seven places, so you don't need to win to earn.
