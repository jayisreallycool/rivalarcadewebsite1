Cosmetics are only worth collecting if you can see them properly. Your avatar is now a full 3D model everywhere it matters.

## Spin, zoom, inspect

In the **wardrobe**, the store's **On you** try-on view and your **profile**, drag with a finger or mouse to spin the avatar all the way round, with inertia. Pinch, scroll or use the +/− buttons to zoom toward the spot under your fingers, and drag up and down to look over the body while zoomed. A reset button puts everything back.

## More detail

The model picked up rounded eyes with catch-lights, brows and a mouth that follow your chosen expression, ears, a nose, rounded shoulders and smoother hair and limbs. Equipped emotes play at rest, and trails swirl around the avatar in their own style — bolts, flames, glitter, bubbles, falling code, rainbow ribbon or void shards.

## Items look like their names

Every item has one feature table that drives both the 3D model and the 2D avatar, so the gear you see in the shop is the gear you see equipped. Hoodies have hoods and drawstrings, cleats have studs, the Inferno Jersey carries its #99, and the Ember Phoenix Wings keep their feathers. All 74 product images were regenerated from the real 3D models.

## Built for every phone

Studio lighting with soft shadows runs on capable GPUs; weaker devices get standard materials at 30 fps, and devices without WebGL 2 keep the 2D avatar.
