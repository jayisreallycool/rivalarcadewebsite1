RivalArcade has 74 cosmetics across ten slots — hair, tops, bottoms, shoes, headwear, eyewear, back gear, trails, emotes and banners. Every one belongs to one of six rarity tiers, and rarity decides how an item looks as much as what it costs.

## The six tiers

| Rarity | Finish | Price (RC) | Availability |
|---|---|---|---|
| Common | Matte | 500–1,000 | Very accessible |
| Uncommon | Gloss | 1,500–3,000 | Accessible |
| Rare | Gloss, double border | 5,000–9,000 | Moderate |
| Epic | Metal, shimmer | 15,000–25,000 | Difficult |
| Legendary | Chrome, sparkle | 40,000–65,000 | Very difficult |
| Mythic | Holo, animated aurora | 75,000–90,000 | Extremely difficult |

Higher tiers move up the finish ladder — matte to gloss to metal to chrome to holo — and get stronger glows and frame effects in the store. Legendary product images get god rays; Mythic items get holographic foil.

## Some highlights

- **Mythic:** Aurora Holo Coat, Exo-Weave Vest, Void Walkers, Apex Crown of Glory, Seraph Halo, Plasma Light Wings, Void Rift trail.
- **Legendary:** Apex Gold Bomber, Diamond Royale Jacket, Aureus Court Cleats, Obsidian Warlord Crown, Ember Phoenix Wings, Rocket Jetpack MK-II, Rainbow Rush trail.
- **Epic:** Inferno Jersey #99, Grav-Boots 3000, Gridlock HUD Visor, Shadow Katana Scabbard, Matrix Rain trail.

## Collections

Items are grouped into themed sets, with one featured in the store each week: **Neon Circuit**, **Inferno**, **Frostbite**, **Royal Gold**, **Void** and **Street Classics**.

## How the store works

The store has a featured strip, the **Mythic Vault**, daily picks that change at 00:00 UTC, a weekly collection, best sellers, rarity filters and sorting. Tap any item for a preview, try it on your 3D avatar in the **On you** view, and confirm before you buy. Locked items can be previewed but never equipped until you own them.

## Cosmetics you can only win

Some items are cup prizes. The champion of each scheduled cup takes that cup's cosmetic — like the Phoenix Crimson Flare trail from the Neon Dash Grand Prix.

## Always cosmetic

No item changes hitboxes, speed or scoring. Rarity is about how you look, never how you play.
