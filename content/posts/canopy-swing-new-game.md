RivalArcade's newest game is all about momentum. In **Canopy Swing** you hook a vine, swing, let go and fly — then do it again before you lose height.

## How it plays

Hold anywhere and your hook gun fires at the vine tip in reach. Let go to fly, keeping every bit of speed you built up. Release on the upswing and you can clear two, three or more vines in one flight.

Hang around too long and a fire wall rolls in from the left, so the only safe place is forward. The run moves through four stages — Slow, Middle, Final and Crazy — and the fire or a single splash in the river ends it.

You see your own 3D avatar from the side, hanging from the vine and reaching for the next one, with the camera leading ahead so the next vine is always on screen. A glowing ring around your hands shows the release window.

## Scoring

- **1 point** for every 2 metres you travel
- **+3** for every berry you grab

Like every RivalArcade game, the scoring is simple on purpose: distance is score, and the bonus pickups are worth taking a line for.

## The Canopy Swing Classic

Canopy Swing has its own scheduled cup, the **Canopy Swing Classic**. Same jungle for everyone — swing the furthest to take first place and the **Jade Spirit Blade**, a straight jian with a tassel for your avatar's back slot.
