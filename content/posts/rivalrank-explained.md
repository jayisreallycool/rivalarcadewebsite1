RivalRank is RivalArcade's competitive ladder. It's the number that tells you — and everyone else — how you stack up in head-to-head play.

## The rating

Every player starts at **1200**. RivalRank is an Elo-style rating: rated 1v1 results move it up or down, and how far it moves depends on who you played. Beat someone rated well above you and you gain more; lose to someone rated below you and you drop more. Beat a lower-rated player and you gain only a little.

Because everyone in a match plays the identical seed, the rating reflects skill at the game rather than luck of the draw.

## Placement

Your first **five** rated matches are placement matches. Your profile shows *Placement x/5* until they're done, then you land in a tier.

## The eight tiers

| Tier | Rating |
|---|---|
| Bronze | below 1150 |
| Silver | 1150+ |
| Gold | 1250+ |
| Platinum | 1350+ |
| Diamond | 1450+ |
| Master | 1550+ |
| Grandmaster | 1700+ |
| Rival Elite | 1850+ |

Reaching Master, Grandmaster and Rival Elite also unlocks achievements worth 1,000, 1,500 and 2,000 Rival Coins.

## Per-game ratings

You also have a rating for each game, so the leaderboards can show who's best at Dunk Rush specifically, not just who plays the most.

## Seasons

Competitive seasons run by calendar quarter. At the start of a new season your rating is **softly reset halfway back toward 1200** — a 1600 becomes 1400, a 1000 becomes 1100. Your old season's final and peak ratings stay in your season history, so past achievements aren't lost.

## How to climb

1. **Practice first.** Practice is free and unlimited, and the seed is random — perfect for learning patterns.
2. **Pick your games.** Your per-game ratings show where you're strongest.
3. **Play the specials.** Swishes, perfect drops and close calls are bonus points — they're usually what separates two good runs.
4. **Mind the clutch.** In timed games the last ten seconds double every point.
