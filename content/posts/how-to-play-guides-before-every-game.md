Twelve games means twelve sets of controls. Starting this week, every RivalArcade game teaches its own controls before you play, right on the ready card.

## What's new on the ready card

Open any game and the card now leads with **How to Play**:

- **Three animated gesture demos.** A fingertip shows the exact move — the flick in Dunk Rush, the swipe in Neon Dash, the drag-and-release in Brick Blitz, the slash in Cyber Reflex, the swap in Gem Surge.
- **The goal in one line.** "Throw every axe into the spinning log. Never hit another axe." No guessing what counts.
- **Colour-coded point chips.** Normal actions, bonuses, gold items, jackpots, ON FIRE ×2, CLUTCH ×2 and penalties each get their own colour, so you can see what's worth chasing at a glance.
- **How the round ends.** Timed, endless, three shields, one clang — it's spelled out before you start.

The full rules are still there under **Full rules** for anyone who wants every detail.

## A Get Ready strip through the countdown

When you tap play, a compact **Get Ready** controls strip stays on screen through 3-2-1 and fades out as the game starts. It's a last-second reminder, not a pop-up you have to dismiss. If your device has reduced-motion turned on, the animations respect it.

## Why it matters for competition

In a 1v1 or a cup you get **one run** on the shared seed. Learning the controls on your first competitive attempt is a bad way to lose. Read the card, take a free practice run or two, then jump into ranked when you're ready.
