When you can switch between twelve games in a minute, the points should mean the same thing in all of them. RivalArcade now uses one scoring language everywhere.

## The rules

- **1 point per action.** A basket, a node, a gem, a brick, a slab, a dodge, a crystal — each normal action is one point.
- **Specials are labelled.** Swishes, bank shots and perfect timing add +1. Gold items are worth 3, jackpots 5. The game tells you on screen when you earn one.
- **ON FIRE ×2.** Five in a row and every point counts double until the streak breaks.
- **CLUTCH ×2.** The last ten seconds of a timed round double every point — and it stacks with ON FIRE.

## Consistent rounds

Timed games all run 60 seconds with clutch time at the end. Endless games such as Brick Blitz and Canopy Swing end on a fail instead.

## Why small numbers are better for competition

Big, inflated scores hide what actually happened. With one point per action, a result breakdown adds up line by line, and you can see exactly where a rival beat you — three more swishes, one fewer red node. The results screen reads from the same scoring book as the game, so the numbers always match.

Old high scores, recent scores and match history were rescaled to the new points automatically, so nobody lost their records.
