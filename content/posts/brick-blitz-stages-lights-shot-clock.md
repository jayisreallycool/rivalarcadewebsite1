Brick Blitz has always rewarded clean aim. This update makes it faster, tenser and better suited to head-to-head play.

## Four seeded stages

Runs now move through four stages, and because they're seeded, every player in the same match sees the same progression:

| Stage | Rows |
|---|---|
| Easy | 1–6 |
| Challenging | 7–14 |
| Hard | 15–24 |
| Extreme | 25+ |

Each stage hardens and packs the rows, adds tough bricks and speeds the balls up.

## The shot clock

A new **shot clock** keeps the pressure on. It starts at nine seconds and tightens to five by the Extreme stage. If it runs out, the game fires for you at your current aim — so there's no stalling your way through a tough board. Volleys fast-forward sooner, a tap jumps to ×3 speed, and rows drop faster.

## Lights that tell you where you are

Stage-coloured lamps hang over the lane and pulse with every break. Bricks shatter with flashes and shockwave rings (gold for tough bricks), and the arena edges, launcher ring and balls re-colour at each new stage with a **STAGE** banner. When the wall is one row from the line, a red warning light starts to pulse. Weaker GPUs get fewer lights so the game stays smooth.

## How to score more

- Quick shots under two seconds earn a bonus point.
- Clearing a full row is +2; clearing the board is a 5-point jackpot.
- Tough bricks and stars are worth 3, gold bricks 5.
