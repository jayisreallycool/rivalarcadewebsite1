Brick Blitz is an aim-and-launch game with no clock. Your run lasts until the creeping wall reaches the red line with no shields left. That makes it one of the deepest games in RivalArcade — and one of the most rewarding to get good at.

## Controls

- **Drag** to aim. Dots show the path of the volley.
- **Let go** to fire.
- **Tap** to fast-forward a volley.

The wall moves while you aim, and a shot clock (nine seconds early on, five by the Extreme stage) fires for you at your current aim if you wait too long.

## Scoring

| Action | Points |
|---|---|
| Brick | 1 |
| Row survived | 1 |
| Tough brick or star | 3 |
| Gold brick | 5 |
| Row clear | +2 |
| Quick shot (under 2 s) | +1 |
| Power-up | +1 |
| Board clear | 5 |

## Power-ups unlock as you go

You start with ten balls. Power-ups unlock one by one as you get deeper, each guaranteed on the row where it debuts and random after that: extra balls, row laser, bomb, column laser, fireball, split, shield and freeze. Shields are what keep a run alive when the wall gets close.

## Five habits that add rows

1. **Bank off the walls.** Steep angles that ride the side walls get behind the front row.
2. **Clear the lowest row first.** The wall reaching the line is the only thing that ends your run.
3. **Shoot quickly when the shot is obvious.** Under two seconds is a free bonus point.
4. **Save the angle for tough bricks.** At 3 points each, they're worth a deliberate line.
5. **Respect the red light.** When it pulses, the wall is one row away — prioritise survival over score.

## Stages

Runs move from Easy (rows 1–6) to Challenging (7–14), Hard (15–24) and Extreme (25+). In a 1v1 both players get the same seeded stages, so the player who handles Hard better usually wins.
